About

I'm a Product Manager in Toronto. I've spent seven years at PolicyMe, a Canadian insurtech, growing from business and operations analyst into a product manager who takes new products from zero to one.

Most recently I built Health & Dental from nothing into a line on track for more than $10M in 2026, launching across two distribution channels and reaching profitable unit economics in its first year.

I like the unglamorous parts: pricing assumptions, renewal logic, the operational reality behind a clean checkout. Good insurance products are mostly good systems, and I care about the ones customers never have to think about.

Before product I sat close to operations, which is still how I work — near the data, near the people handling the edge cases, and skeptical of anything that only works in a demo.

    How I work

    • Start with the problem, not the feature.A feature request is usually a summary of something that went wrong upstream. It's worth finding out what.
    • Make ambiguity concrete.On a vague project the first deliverable is a written definition, not a design. Most disagreement turns out to be about wording nobody has pinned down yet.
    • Understand the operational reality behind the interface.Every screen has a person behind it processing exceptions. If I haven't sat with them, I don't understand the product yet.
